the pipeline

One path, code to part.

01 / DEFINE

Define

Geometry as parametric code. Change a number, regenerate the part.

02 / SIMULATE

Simulate

Physics sim proves the mechanism and the motion before any metal moves.

03 / SCHEDULE

Schedule

An operation graph sequences the cells and cuts the cycle time.

04 / TOOLPATH

Toolpath

CAM turns each step into arm motion, checked for reach and clearance.

05 / BUILD

Build

Tool-changing robot cells form, cut, press, and assemble the real part.

06 / VERIFY

Verify

The finished part is measured against the CAD. Out of tolerance never ships.

the shop floor

Processes, on tap.

wire

Wire forming

CNC bends from a program. Springback is calibrated per spool, so the first part is right.

sheet

Sheet & foil forming

Fold flat stock into stiff profiles — or layer-form solid parts from cheap foil.

arms

Tool-changing arms

One arm, many tools: shear, press, gripper, glue — swapped in seconds at the dock.

press

Press & insert

A self-reacting C-frame seats bearings and inserts with hundreds of newtons — and the arm feels almost none of it.

assembly

Adhesive & assembly

Two arms coordinate: one holds the joint, the other lays the bead, and holds until it sets.

lom

Layer forming

Stack, bond, and cut sheet into a finished solid — tooling-free, straight from the slice stack.

manufacturing for design

Don't design a part. Sweep a space.

Design for Manufacturing bends the design to fit the machine.
We bend the machine to fit the design.

When simulate-and-verify costs almost nothing, you stop guessing a single part and sweep a whole family — every variant built and checked in software, the constraints picking the survivor. You don't hit a spec; you map the envelope around it. Something traditional shops can't afford, because there each try burns a real tool and a real setup.
