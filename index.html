the pipeline

One path, code to assembly.

Today: define, simulate and verify in software. Building it for you is the roadmap.

now01 / DEFINE

Define

The whole assembly as parametric code: parts, mates and motion. Change a number, regenerate all of it.

now02 / SIMULATE

Simulate

Physics sim proves the mechanism and the motion before any metal moves.

now03 / VERIFY

Verify

Clearance, interference, wall thickness and print orientation checked against the design before anything is made.

roadmap04 / SCHEDULE + TOOLPATH

Plan the build

An operation graph sequences robot cells; CAM turns each step into arm motion, checked for reach.

roadmap05 / BUILD

Build

Tool-changing robot cells form, cut, press, and put the real assembly together.

roadmap06 / MEASURE

Measure

The finished assembly is measured against the CAD, and the measurements feed back into the simulation.

Where we're headed: the shop floor.

These are the processes we're developing so the same code that proves a design can also build it. None of them is offered as a service yet.

wire

Wire forming

CNC bends from a program, with springback calibrated per spool so the first part comes out right.

sheet

Sheet & foil forming

Fold flat stock into stiff profiles — or layer-form solid parts from cheap foil.

arms

Tool-changing arms

One arm, many tools: shear, press, gripper, glue — swapped in seconds at the dock.

press

Press & insert

A self-reacting C-frame seats bearings and inserts with hundreds of newtons — and the arm feels almost none of it.

assembly

Adhesive & assembly

Two arms coordinate: one holds the joint, the other lays the bead, and holds until it sets.

lom

Layer forming

Stack, bond, and cut sheet into a finished solid — tooling-free, straight from the slice stack.

manufacturing for design

Don't design a part. Sweep a space.

Design for Manufacturing bends the design to fit the machine.
We bend the machine to fit the design.

When simulate-and-verify costs almost nothing, you stop guessing a single design and sweep a whole family — every variant built and checked in software, the constraints picking the survivor. You don't hit a spec; you map the envelope around it. Something traditional shops can't afford, because there each try burns a real tool and a real setup.

The simulation is a cache of reality.

A simulation is only as good as its numbers. Every parameter is traced to a source — a datasheet, a paper, a measurement — or flagged as an assumption until it's measured on real hardware. You always know which results are proven and which are provisional.
